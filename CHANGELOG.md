# Changelog

## v0.1 (first public release)

- Modelled preamp, power amp and speaker load, plus Neural Amp Modeler (`.nam`) captures.
- Modelled cabinets with placeable microphones; 33 bundled cabinet impulses.
- Circuit-modelled drives and fuzzes; compressor, limiter, wah, EQ, modulation, pitch, synth, delay and reverb pedals.
- 24 bundled rooms (SPACE) with input or room drift.
- Two amps in stereo or blended, looper, tuner, 100 presets.
- Import `.nam`, `.wav` and zips by drag and drop.
