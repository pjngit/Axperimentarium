Bundled impulses
----------------
The cabinets in "Axperimentarium cabinets.zip" and the rooms in
"Axperimentarium spaces.zip" are new impulses, rebuilt from measured
statistics of published impulse responses (frequency response, per-band
decay, echo pattern, stereo width) onto fresh noise; no recorded samples are
included. Most rooms are rebuilt from the EchoThief Impulse Response Library
by Chris Warren (echothief.com).
